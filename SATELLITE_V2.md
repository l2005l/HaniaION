# Satellite Mission Control v2

The page at `/satellite` includes:

1. Globe — a self-contained interactive globe centered near Israel with public observation satellites, trails and click details.
2. Israel Sky — a sky dome calculated from each satellite ground point and altitude. Center means overhead; rim means horizon.
3. Mission Control — current counts, next pass, data-source state, world ground tracks and a 90-minute timeline.

The system does not claim that a sensor is active or collecting imagery. “Visible” means estimated geometric opportunity.
